DARÉ · Cast and crew

Identity cards for the people of the show

One card per person. A picture or a clip in the window, their name and their part beneath it.

An identity document for DARÉ: the paper, the photograph, the stamp, the machine-readable line along the bottom. Make a card for everyone in the cast, the band and the crew, and post them one at a time as the announcements. Every word on it can be changed here.

Cards are kept in this browser, so you can come back and change them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Save a still as PNG, or record the card as a short clip, with the person's own footage playing in the window if that is what you dropped in.
