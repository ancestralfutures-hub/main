Family,

I know I said this the other day, but from the bottom of my heart, I'm so grateful to those of you who attended the opening of DARÉ. It means a lot to me and the team that you came through.

I'm still in awe of the support you guys have shown, but as my producer Kelsea would want me to say, there are still many ways you can support this dream.

If you haven't already, pop in to experience the full 40-minute soundscape at Zimbabwe House. We've got showings every hour, on the hour, until Thursday 22 October.

Come and listen
 

So family, will we be seeing you again?

I mentioned briefly, but I also want to give you guys the heads-up that you can see the installation come to life as a live show on 6 and 7 November at Bernie Grant Arts Centre.

This won't be another listening session. We'll be bringing DARÉ to the stage with live performances, music and immersive visuals, featuring artists Zola Marcelle, Steam Down, Afronaut Zu, Theon Cross and more who also feature on the project.

During this, you'll get a real feel for my interpretation of a daré, a gathering, a space for reflection that hopefully raises your vibrations when it's over.
