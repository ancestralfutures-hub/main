Family,

We're looking forward to welcoming you tomorrow evening for the private view of DARÉ: A Sonic Portrait by Shumba Maasai, presented by Ancestral Futures in partnership with the Embassy of Zimbabwe.

Join us for an evening of art, sound, conversation and gathering as we celebrate this new iteration of DARÉ.

 

What to expect

Doors open at 8pm, with alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks available, alongside light refreshments kindly provided by Tasty African Food.

At around 8:45pm we gather in the library for TINYMAN in conversation with Shumba Maasai, on the history of DARÉ, its curatorial vision, and how this work connects us to memory, heritage and each other.

Following this there will be music from Charisse C, founder of Abantu, alongside the immersive sonic experience of DARÉ.

You're welcome to move between the library and the DARÉ installation, spending time in the spaces at your own pace.

 

The Daré experience, in short

On the night, the DARÉ experience is open to preview in its short format, so everyone can step inside.

The full experience is an hour of your own, booked by the slot, every day from 15 to 22 October, 1pm to 8pm. Please come back and book a full experience slot.

Access information

We want everyone to have the information they need to make an informed decision about attending.

Physical access. There are two steps at the entrance to Zimbabwe House, so unfortunately the event is not wheelchair accessible. Once inside, the floors are flat. There are a further two steps between the library and the DARÉ installation rooms. Chairs with backs can be made available on request; please let us know in advance if you need one.

Sound and sensory. There will be music and immersive sound throughout the evening. DARÉ is a shoes-off and phone-free experience; this applies only inside the DARÉ installation, not in the library or other gathering areas. Earplugs, face masks and hand sanitiser will be available on request.

Access requests. If you have any access requirements, questions about the space, or would like us to reserve a chair with a back, please get in touch through Eventbrite before attending.

 

A little collective care

We're looking forward to gathering with you, and we also want to look after one another. If you're feeling unwell, particularly with symptoms of an infectious illness, please stay home and take care of yourself. There will be other opportunities to experience DARÉ.

Thank you for being part of this gathering.
