Family, it's tomorrow.

Here is who will be on stage with me at Bernie Grant Arts Centre for Daré: A Live Encounter with Sound.

Vocals

Afronaut Zu

A Nigerian vocalist and multidisciplinary artist whose voice moves between funk, soul and Afrobeat. Rooted in his African heritage, his music is about resilience, spirituality and empowerment.

Wind Conjurer

Ahnansé

A London saxophonist, composer and founder of Steam Down, drawing on jazz, improvisation and sounds from across the African diaspora. He brings breath, rhythm and improvisation into the room.

Words

Lex Amor

A North London rapper, producer and DJ, one of the most distinctive voices in UK music. Her work moves between introspection, storytelling and rhythm, on identity, community and everyday life.

Voice

Zola Marcelle

A Shona and Sotho, London-based vocalist and multidisciplinary artist. Her practice asks questions of Being, the Divine, and what we do with these ever-shifting realities.

Poet and Narrator

MA.MOYO

Born in Mashonaland, Belinda Zhawi is a London and Marseille-based poet, literary and sound artist. She is our conduit between this world and the next, guiding us through memory, ancestry and the unseen.
