Yes yes people!

First off, much love to everyone who came down to Daré at Zimbabwe House last week. It was amazing to welcome you all into the space, to gather, listen and commune together. I'm still finding the words to describe what we shared, but I'm grateful to everyone who came through and brought their energy into the space.

Now, we take Daré somewhere new.

Next Saturday, 7th November, we bring Daré to Bernie Grant Arts Centre in Tottenham, North London, this time in the form of live experimental theatre.

The auditorium at Bernie Grant Arts Centre, Tottenham

From the moment you enter, we want to take you somewhere.

Through live music, poetry, storytelling and performance, we'll explore what Daré can become when it moves beyond the installation and into a living, breathing encounter between everyone in the room.

I'll be performing the music live alongside a band and some very special friends, with more details to follow. We're putting our whole hearts into this one, as always, and working to create something truly extraordinary.

Family, I need your support on this one.

If you experienced Daré at Zimbabwe House, this is another chapter of the journey, and I'd love for you to be part of it.

Buy a ticket, come through and tell a friend to tell a friend. Bring the younger generation too. It's important that they get to experience and engage with this work, and that we continue creating spaces where generations can come together.
