# Email

One for every day, as asked. Each is three or four lines: a daily email
has to be short or it stops being opened.

**The honest note, once.** Eighteen sends in eighteen days to an event
list is a lot, and unsubscribes usually bite around day four or five. The
ones marked **★** are the ones I would send if it were six emails rather
than eighteen. The rest are written and ready if you want the daily beat.

Build them from `assets/email/three-weeks.html`: swap the headline, the
line under it and Shumba's note, leave everything else.
