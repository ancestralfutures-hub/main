Yes Yes People,

Shumba here. Last time I wrote, I said Daré was entering its next chapter and that London would be the first stop. I can now tell you where, and tickets are open.

From 15 to 22 October we will be at Zimbabwe House, 429 Strand.

It has been over a year in the making: the pilot in east London in August 2025, and then the time in Zimbabwe with Gyaan and the Centre of Knowledge and Equity, taking nine practitioners around the country and to my ancestral village. All of that has found its way into what you will walk into.

Shoes off. No phones. Come as you are, and give yourself time in there.

Thank you for staying with me.

Big Love,
Shumba Maasai ++
